Knowledge workers increasingly delegate search, reading, and composition to Generative AI agents. What returns is fluent and citation-bearing, but the basis on which it was composed, the corpus that was available for consultation, the passage relied upon, and the path from passage to claim, is discarded when the session closes. We name this condition delegation without durable provenance, and distinguish it from hallucination and from model opacity: it obtains even where the output is accurate, because the deficit lies in the record rather than in the output. Existing responses each address one part. Attribution assesses a claim against its source at the moment of composition; data provenance computes a record from a transformation whose semantics are known; scholarly policy states what must exist. None addresses whether the basis is still reachable when a worker is called upon to defend the claim. We argue that evidentiary standing is not settled at composition but must be sustained for as long as the claim stands, which makes evidence something a work environment keeps rather than only something a system is checked against. Following a design science approach, we develop a design framework for such an environment and instantiate it in ProRead, a local-first reading and research environment that operates over a corpus the knowledge worker holds.
