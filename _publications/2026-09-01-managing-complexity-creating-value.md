Knowledge workers increasingly manage task portfolios containing heterogeneous and interconnected products, customers, and activities. Prior information systems (IS) research has often labeled component variety and interdependence as complexity, although recent complexity-science-informed work distinguishes complicatedness - the proliferation of interconnected components - from complexity that emerges through contingent, ad hoc, and nonlinear interactions. We build on that distinction while problematizing a further assumption: mechanisms that tame complexity by restructuring interactions among organizational units need not operate equivalently when the focal system is an individual knowledge worker's task portfolio. At the individual level, complicatedness can create valuable variety as well as processing demands, and IS can respond either by imposing predetermined structure or by expanding the actor's repertoire of context-contingent responses. We therefore theorize that deterministic CRM feature use increasingly constrains the performance returns to portfolio complicatedness, whereas probabilistic GenAI feature use increasingly augments the worker's response variety and raises those returns. We test these predictions using agent- and deal-level data from a U.S. Insurtech platform and a quasi-experimental shift-share identification strategy that addresses endogenous portfolio complicatedness and technology use. The results support both directional hypotheses. CRM negatively moderates the performance effects of product and customer complicatedness, whereas GenAI positively moderates them. Nonlinear patterns further show that structure provides a buffer when customer complicatedness reaches levels at which additional variety begins to reduce performance. The study extends IS complexity research by distinguishing interaction restructuring in organizational systems from how complicatedness is managed within individual work-portfolio systems. It shows that IS can create value by either imposing structure on how workers handle complicatedness or expanding their capacity to respond to it.
