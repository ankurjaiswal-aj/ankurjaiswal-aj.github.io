Research Experience

  • Research Associate, Information Systems Mar 2020 – Aug 2022
    Indian School of Business, Hyderabad
    • Conducted empirical analysis on Fortune 500 firms' digital endowments, resilience to shocks, and IT diversity, using causal methods like difference-in-differences and panel regressions.
    • Built datasets from sources including Harte Hanks, Compustat, and CRSP; created measures for digital assets, organizational elasticity, CAPM, and Fama-French market returns.
    • Developed methods sections for papers on diversity's role in mitigating shocks and IT-enabled innovation speed, using DID, FGLS, and Heckman models.
    • Contributed to 3 conference papers at HICSS 2022 and 1 book chapter.
  • Research Assistant Mar 2019 – Feb 2020
    Research and Information System for Developing Countries (RIS), New Delhi
    • Worked with RIS, a think tank under India's Ministry of External Affairs, on policy recommendations for enhancing medicinal plant exports.
    • Used qualitative and quantitative methods, including snowball sampling, to collect primary data.
    • Conducted fieldwork in remote Indian villages, interviewing farmers, traders, wholesalers, and manufacturers across the medicinal plant value chain.
  • Research Assistant, Strategy May 2018 – Mar 2019
    Indian Institute of Management, Calcutta
    • Analyzed corporate governance, business group strategies, CSR, and diversification impacts using polychoric PCA, panel data, and two-stage regression models.
    • Developed Principal-Agent conflict measures, validated foreign market entry data, and used scarce event logistic regression to assess the effects of institutional distance on entry decisions.
    • Created an Institutional Development Index (from WCY, ICRG, IMF data) and tested CSR-performance relationships using ESG data and instrumental variable regressions.
    • Awarded best track proposal at the SMS Special Conference, for research on diversification and organizational performance.
