I study how Generative AI, predictive AI, and enterprise CRM systems create business value for organizations — using applied econometrics and causal inference on large-scale firm and platform data.

I am a PhD student in Information Systems at the McCombs School of Business, The University of Texas at Austin. My research examines the business value of IT, with a focus on Generative AI, online platforms, and CRM/Salesforce systems. Before UT Austin, I worked as a research associate at the Indian School of Business, where I studied how firms' digital endowments shaped their resilience to shocks such as the COVID-19 pandemic.

My current working paper studies how Generative AI tools affect deal-closure times and business value in insurance policy sales within CRM systems. My earlier work — presented at ICIS and HICSS, and published as a book chapter — looked at business intelligence systems, multicultural firms, and organizational resilience during extreme shocks.
